# journals/

Each week, you write a short reflection with your **review agent** — what you accomplished, the decisions you made, and what you learned. Those reflections are saved here, one dated file per week.

Two reasons they matter: they keep you (and your teacher) oriented as the project grows, and they accumulate the raw material — in your own words — that you'll draw on for your competition application later. Your review agent prompts you; you do the writing.
