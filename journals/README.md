# journals/

Each week, you write a short reflection with your **Review Agent** — what you accomplished, the decisions you made, and what you learned. Those reflections are saved here, one dated file per week.

Two reasons they matter: they keep you (and your teacher) oriented as the project grows, and they accumulate the raw material — in your own words — that you'll draw on for your competition application later. Your Review Agent prompts you; you do the writing.

Your weekly reflection also carries two things the competition specifically asks for: a **prompt log** — the prompts you gave the agents that week — and a short **note of where AI helped** (which code it wrote, which analysis it assisted). The Society for Science's AI-use rules require this record for AI-written code and analysis, and keeping it weekly means you're never reconstructing it under deadline two years later. When the data-policy check runs (early on, when you're choosing a dataset), its finding goes here too, in your own words: which source, what its terms say, and how you're allowed to work with it.
