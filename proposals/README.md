# proposals/

When a research cycle's plan is complete, your mentor compiles it into a single **proposal document** here — one per cycle (`cycle-1-proposal.md`, and later `cycle-2-…`). It gathers the sections you've already written plus short forward-looking paragraphs about how you'll collect and analyze your data.

This proposal is what goes to your teacher for sign-off, and it's also the start of your IRB application if your project needs one. Approving it is the moment your cycle "seals" — your predictions lock in, and you move from planning to running your study.
