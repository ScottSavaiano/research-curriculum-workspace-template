# versions/

Every time your paper syncs, a dated snapshot of it is saved here. It's a safety net — nothing you write is ever lost, and a bad save can always be rolled back to a known-good version.

It's also a quiet record of your paper growing over the year, in your own writing — which is useful evidence that the work is genuinely yours. You don't manage this folder; your agents write to it automatically on each sync.
